# Ventura visual assets

The active Ventura operations floor and map are original clean-room assets:

- `ventura/ventura-operations-floor-source.png` — generated from the Ventura art brief recorded in `docs/assets/PROVENANCE.md`.
- `ventura/ventura-operations-floor.png` — runtime-sized derivative of that source.
- `maps/ventura-operations.tmj` — original Ventura collision, spawn-point, and zone map.
- Role portraits and walk frames are drawn at runtime by `scene/office/portraitArt.ts`.

No LimeZu art, Office character sheets, Office maps, or recolors are included in the distributable source. Historic upstream versions remain available through Git history and are covered by their original terms; they are not part of an Ventura build.
